## Validation chain (post-fix)

`POST /api/v1/reservations/quote` và `POST /api/v1/reservations/confirm` validate theo thứ tự:

1. **Cafe mở cửa ngày đó** (`CafeSchedule.IsClosed`) → 400 `CafeScheduleClosedForPlayDate`.
2. **Preferred times hợp lệ với `CafeSchedule`** (G3) → 400 `PreferredStartBeforeOpen` / `PreferredEndAfterClose`.
3. **`playDate ∈ [today, today+7]`** (G2) → 400 `PlayDateOutOfRange`.
4. **`scheduledStartTime > now`** (G11) → 400 `StartTimeInPast`.
5. **`scheduledEndTime > scheduledStartTime`** → 400 `PreferredTimesMustDiffer`.
6. **Duration 30 phút ≤ d ≤ 12 giờ** (G7/G8) → 400 `DurationTooLong(12)` / `DurationTooShort(30)`.
7. **Overnight rule**: nếu overnight, `endDate == startDate + 1` → 400 `LateNightMustEndNextDay`.
8. **Same-day rule**: nếu không overnight, `endDate == startDate` → 400 `ReservationEndTimeDifferentDay`.
9. **Defensive assertion** (G13): `DateOnly.FromDateTime(scheduledStartTime) == playDate` (chỉ fail khi `BuildScheduledStartEndFromPreferred` có bug).
10. **Buffer** `recruitmentDeadline - now()`:
    - `< 60 phút` → 400 `BufferTooShort` (BR-LOBBY-01b).
    - `60–120 phút` → 200 với `bufferWarning = true` (BR-LOBBY-01c).
    - `≥ 120 phút` → 200 (BR-LOBBY-01a).
    - **Demo bypass** (G16): `X-Bypass-Demo-Locks: true` header → skip toàn bộ buffer check.
11. **User eligibility** (BR-USER-LIMIT-*, BR-NEW-05, BR-RISK-04, BR-NEW-10, BR-DEMO-*).
12. **`availableBalance ≥ finalDeposit`** (BR-DEPOSIT-01).
13. **Seat + Game copy availability** (BR-RESERVATION-01/02, Serializable retry tối đa 3 lần, G12).
14. **Atomic transaction**: trừ BVC + insert Reservation + insert Lobby + hold seat + hold game.
